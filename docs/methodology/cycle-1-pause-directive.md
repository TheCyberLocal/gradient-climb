# Research Cycle 1 pause directive

On 2026-09-11 the project owner explicitly replaced the open-ended research
mandate with a bounded stabilization and handoff task. This directive governs
the present release; the original research brief remains historical scope.

- Preserve active governed experiments unchanged through legitimate finalization.
  Do not launch queued/new research merely to finish an old plan.
- Fix actual architecture, runtime, provenance, test or presentation defects.
  Do not introduce new algorithms, calibration, reward campaigns or major features.
- Inspect repository/remote state, running processes, plans, canonical records,
  ignored drafts, CI, dependencies, reports, hypotheses, findings and operations.
- Document demonstrated results separately from implemented capabilities and plans.
  Nominal surrogate distance is not actual Hill Climb Racing distance or transfer.
- Preserve distance-based semantics and results. The next research cycle must
  investigate versioned distance/score/time/recovery objectives. Raw score alone
  may reward spectacular but strategically fatal behavior.
- Refresh every original completion gate as SATISFIED, PARTIAL, MISSING or
  DEFERRED; implementation and planned experiments are not empirical outcomes.
- Adjudicate hypotheses as SUPPORTED, NOT_SUPPORTED, INCONCLUSIVE, SUPERSEDED or
  PAUSED, stating missing evidence. Create durable source-linked findings.
- Create a canonical resumption plan with status, prerequisites, intended evidence,
  implementation locations and completion criteria for each remaining major task.
- Inventory important local evidence and drafts. Preserve or explicitly defer
  unfinished drafts; no critical architecture may exist only in conversation.
- Refresh README, interim report, notebooks and resume checkpoint to the final
  evidence cutoff. Record model/config/profile/reward versions and key run IDs.
- Verify finalized run integrity, complete pytest/Ruff checks, environment and
  dashboard behavior including missing research categories. Live game tests stay
  separate from CI. Do not rerun expensive experiments just to verify artifacts.
- Classify every registered/dispatched plan at the pause; preserve historical
  plan files and record completed/partial/never-started/interrupted/deferred status.
- Keep version 0.1.0a1 unless correctness requires otherwise. Push a clean dev,
  require green CI on its exact SHA, verify main has not changed independently,
  merge under repository governance, push main and verify remote state and CI.
- Preserve dev. Once main holds the stabilized cycle, stop development.

The final handoff must identify starting/final SHAs, branch synchronization,
cleanliness and CI; summarize stable capabilities, measured simulator and native
results separately, unproven capabilities, limitations, remaining reward research,
artifact locations and the exact first resumption task.
