# Four-pose visual construction screen

`scripts/run_visual_pose_screen.py --project-root . --protocol experiments/definitions/cycle-3-visual-pose-screen-001.json --replay research/datasets/fbafca5b-visual-pose-labels-v1.json` publishes the single registered screen after labels, source and tests are committed. Do not dispatch from an edited checkout. A canonical attempt, including a failed attempt, consumes this protocol; further work requires a separately registered successor.

The publisher verifies label source order, timestamps, protocol hashes, post-registration review receipts, independent continuity IDs and the whole-session partition ledger. It freezes the ledger, protocol, replay, fixed perception profile and review references before assembling source windows. Exactly four selected source images are decoded and measured, and exactly four original procedural poses are rendered. Required integrity checks additionally hash the sealed source and reviewed image spans. Four decodes does not mean four filesystem reads.

Source RGB and rendered RGB use the existing pixel measurer and screen feature bridge with a reset for every sparse pose. HUD, OS-action and episode context stay unavailable. Landmark labels are diagnostic metadata and never enter actor pixels or features. The retained actor PNG is copied from the exact post-observation array; publication performs no extra render.

Every source operation and render operation is journaled with flush and fsync. Failed runs retain the completed prefix, any produced PNGs, counters, inherited costs, failure reason and uncompleted pose indices. The journal is registered before the recorder seals either success or failure. There are no evaluation records, episodes, physics steps, policy decisions or optimizer updates.

Discrepancies compare uncertain manual construction labels with visible pixel hypotheses. Body comparison uses the polygon area centroid and continuous vertex bounds; the detector reports a color-component centroid and half-open pixel-edge bounds. Wheel association minimizes total center distance for a two-wheel pair without assigning front/rear identity. Terrain comparison uses the visible lower turf/material boundary only within labeled strips and outside declared occlusion, with no interpolation across gaps. Missing comparisons remain null. These quantities do not establish independent accuracy, physical fidelity, full directed orientation, world scale or competence. Tire and terrain appearance remain broad original approximations.

The 120-second function-entry budget is checked between operations, so an indivisible operation may overrun before detection. Imports, partition preflight and final sealing have the explicitly recorded scopes; recorder resources have their own sampling window. The 32-MiB run-output allowance includes copied evidence and reserves 1 MiB for recorder finalization. PNG and report writes are checked before publication. Rendering, source decode, perception and PNG publication timers are separate, nested costs, not additive to whole-function elapsed time. Capture and earlier construction attempts remain inherited evidence; unmeasured annotation, human practice and development costs remain unknown.
