These recordings can guide terrain, bridge, camera and failure analysis after
reviewing game version, vehicle, upgrades, editing and time continuity. A
Moonlander run cannot establish the Hill Climber's action response. Previously
unseen vehicles or maps also do not establish that they are unlocked on the
current installation. Inspected footage becomes construction knowledge; it
cannot silently become an untouched reader or policy qualification set.

The current synchronized recordings retain observed Left/Right states. The
archive has no verified synchronized control stream. Visible pedal graphics may
support a separately validated visual proxy, but cannot be assigned exact key
states, delivery timing or human reaction labels by assumption.

Any viewing, annotation, extraction, fitting or pretraining that uses this archive
must appear in the consuming method's prior lineage. Original human practice,
recording compute and gameplay costs remain unknown. Video duration and views do
not supply those missing costs. No media was downloaded or copied into Git.
