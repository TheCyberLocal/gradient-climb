## Governing interpretation

> **Episodes measure experience. Compute measures cost. Wall-clock time measures rapidity. Real-game capability determines whether the learning mattered.**

The [learning-efficiency methodology](../../docs/methodology/learning-efficiency.md)
defines rapid learning as elapsed training time to a frozen policy's independently
evaluated real capability. Compute and experience costs remain separate. New
registrations must name the threshold, clock boundary, hardware/resource scope,
experience accounting, prior-training class and evaluation procedure. Unreached
thresholds are censored; absent eligible evaluations are not evaluable. Neither
case receives an invented finite learning time.

Cycle 1/2 records, tags, clocks and metrics keep their original semantics. Their
surrogate metres, paused native readings and failed studies are not reclassified
as outcomes of the new protocol. The historical base is
`610dc48dc601f878086053f900ee7a005ccea821`; the last research operation used clean
source `1c80c3265a2a296db384d9b3ec44de87fa915553`. Final integration commits add the
closing documentation and verification receipt to that tested source.
